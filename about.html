About

Hey there. I’m Candi Barrett.

I established Geaux To Notary, LLC in 2025 to better serve the community by providing accessible and affordable notary services.

The Lord has truly blessed me beyond all measure, and I intentionally try to be a blessing to others.

I am a civil law notary, commissioned in Jefferson Parish with statewide jurisdiction. I entered the legal field in 2012 and received an Associate of Arts and Paralegal Certification from Tulane University in 2016.

I am a mother of five wonderful children. I have a phenomenal support system in my parents, who instilled in me a love for the Lord, a strong work ethic, and a passion for serving others.

As a lifelong resident of Louisiana with strong roots in the community, I strive to provide affordable notary services and top-tier customer service.
